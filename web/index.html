    What this handles

    Standard pads, straight tracks, through vias and one closed Edge.Cuts outline. Rounded pads keep their corner radius for hole clearance, and routing uses the enclosing rectangle. Keepouts that restrict tracks or vias, cutouts, custom pads and offset holes are rejected.

    Project net classes and supported DRC constraints apply when you supply a .kicad_pro. Custom .kicad_dru files are not supported. DSN files carry their own rules, net classes and layer settings, and their preview shows routing geometry without source-editor artwork. Download the SES session to bring routing back into the source editor.

    Zone definitions survive routing but cached fills do not. Press B in KiCad to refill. Vias in SMD pads stay off unless you enable them and your fabricator supports the process. Optimization is disabled. Inspect the result and run KiCad DRC before manufacturing.
