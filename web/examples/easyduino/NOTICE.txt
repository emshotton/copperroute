uno.kicad_pcb and uno.kicad_pro are unmodified copies of:
  Atmega328p Arduino Uno/Easyduino_Atmega.kicad_pcb
  Atmega328p Arduino Uno/Easyduino_Atmega.kicad_pro
nano.kicad_pcb and nano.kicad_pro are unmodified copies of:
  Atmega328p Arduino Nano/Easyduino_Atmega_Nano.kicad_pcb
  Atmega328p Arduino Nano/Easyduino_Atmega_Nano.kicad_pro
Only the filenames are shortened for distribution.

These are community Arduino-compatible recreations, not official Arduino
products. No endorsement by Arduino, CERN, or the original contributors is
claimed. The web application code is separately licensed.

Downloads produced by the demo include a dated modification notice in the
board's title block. The router replaces tracks and vias and clears zone fill
caches; users must refill zones and verify the result in KiCad.
