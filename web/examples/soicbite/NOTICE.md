## Files in this directory

`soicbite.kicad_pcb` is the board from the source at that commit, cleaned by the PCBench
scripts under `Scripts/Data_cleaning`. Only the filenames are shortened for
distribution. It is provided under the same license as the source.

Downloads produced by the demo include a dated modification notice in the board's title
block. The router replaces tracks and vias and clears zone fill caches; users must refill
zones and verify the result in KiCad.
